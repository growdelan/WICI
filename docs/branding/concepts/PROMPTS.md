## 01-witka

Plik: 01-witka.png

Prompt:

Use case: logo-brand. Design one polished logo concept presentation for WICI, a Polish local situational awareness map app aggregating official warnings, weather, transport and public communications. Name comes from Polish 'rozpuszczać wici', spreading word / sending summons, with an old-cultural reference, interpreted in contemporary graphic design. Existing UI colors deep ink #193338, teal #14695e, warm white #f6f8f8. Flat vector-like identity design, crisp edges, sophisticated restrained graphic designer work. Must work as a small app icon and a horizontal header lockup. No gradients, no 3D, no shadows, no stock mockups, no sirens, shields, exclamation triangles, no coats of arms. Render a clean landscape concept sheet on warm white: a large primary symbol and wordmark centered in upper two thirds with abundant negative space, and a discrete bottom row with standalone symbol, single-color black version and reversed white-on-dark small tile. Exact main wordmark text "wici" lowercase, carefully kerned custom contemporary sans-serif. No slogans or other text. Make the logo genuinely distinctive, simple and implementable as SVG later.
Concept WITKA: a stylized forked willow twig as historical carrier of a call or message. Reduce to 3 or 4 bold tapered strokes, a short diagonal stem with just two branching ends, subtly suggesting a lowercase w without forcing it. No leaves as separate decorations, no botanical illustration, no eco brand feel. Elegant asymmetric compact mark, teal. Pair with a warm humanist sans wordmark in deep ink. Historical memory conveyed by the twig silhouette, modern situational app conveyed by clarity.
